Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Spendly exists to help you understand your own spending — not to build a profile of you for anyone else. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

1.Data We Collect

We collect only what the service needs in order to function. This falls into three groups:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password, provided when you register.
  • Expense records — the amount, category, date, and description of each expense you choose to log.
  • Basic technical data — a session cookie to keep you signed in, and standard server logs such as request times and error traces.

We do not connect to your bank, ask for card or account numbers, or import transactions automatically. Every expense in Spendly is there because you entered it yourself.

2.How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the service you signed up for — nothing more. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your session active.
  • Display your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Apply the date and category filters you select.
  • Diagnose errors and keep the application running reliably.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3.Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved, so they cannot be read back by us or by anyone with access to the database.

Your records are kept for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual expenses at any time from within the application, and deleting your account removes your associated records from our active systems. Copies may persist in routine backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.Third Party Services

Spendly is built to keep external dependencies to a minimum. The application currently loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page renders. That request may expose your IP address and browser details to Google under their own privacy terms.

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or social media embeds. Your expense records are never transmitted to any third party service.

If we introduce additional third party services in future — such as hosting, email delivery, or error monitoring — we will update this section to name them and describe what they receive.

5.Your Rights

You can view and correct your account details from your profile, and you can export or delete your expense records at any time. If you would like a copy of the data associated with your account, or want it erased entirely, contact us using the details below.

6.Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or in applicable law. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be communicated through the application or by email where appropriate.

7.Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, about what data we hold, or about a request to access or delete it, please reach out through the contact details provided within the application. Your use of Spendly is also governed by our Terms and Conditions.
